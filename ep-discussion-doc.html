The Core Shift

A funding strategy and an income strategy are two entirely different objectives.

⬆ Bank Phase — Going Up the Mountain
Volatility is manageable.
Each year, your full compensation is contributed to the structure. A bank finances the income tax on that contribution — nothing comes out of pocket. The loan balance grows at the bank rate while the policy compounds on the full gross amount.

The Key Mechanic
One continuous loan balance runs the whole way through. It does not reset at refinance — only the rate steps down from the bank rate to the carrier's participating loan rate, right when income begins.
Why It Works
The full gross compensation — not the after-tax remainder — compounds inside the policy from day one. A 0% floor means market losses never reduce that principal, uncorrelated to the funding source.

Step by Step

Enhanced Pension finances the tax on every contribution and deploys the full compensation into a tax-free compounding asset.

The Structure, Step by Step
1
Annual Contribution & Bank Financing
Each contribution year, your full compensation is committed to the structure. A bank simultaneously finances the income tax owed on that contribution. Nothing is paid out of pocket.
2
Funding Phase (10 Annual Contributions)
The full compensation funds a tax-free index life insurance asset each year. The tax-free asset begins accumulating from day one, protected by a 0% floor.
3
Refinance to the Insurance Company
At the refinance year, the bank loan is retired via a participating asset loan at the carrier rate. The balance carries over intact — it does not reset. The asset continues compounding on its full value.
4
Tax-Free Income Phase
Income is taken as participating policy loans — tax-free, stacked on the same loan balance at the carrier rate, and retired from the death benefit. You control the timing and the amount, for life.
Four Structural Advantages
💰
Full Compensation Goes to Work
The entire annual compensation — not the after-tax portion — compounds inside the tax-free asset. The amount that would have gone to the IRS now earns for you instead.
🛡️
0% Floor — No Down Years
When the index has a negative year, the asset credits 0% — not a negative return. Market losses cannot reduce your index-linked principal. Kai's sequence risk problem is structurally addressed.
🔗
One Continuous Loan Balance
The loan does not reset at refinance — only the rate changes, from the bank rate to the participating loan rate, exactly when income begins.
📋
No RMDs — Ever
Life insurance is not subject to Required Minimum Distributions. Income is taken on your schedule — not the IRS's.
