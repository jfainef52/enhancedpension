What We're Recommending — And Why
1
Finance the tax on every contribution — don't pay it out of pocket

A commercial bank finances the income tax owed on each year's contribution as it's made. No check written to the IRS. The loan is secured by the policy — not your other assets — and is retired later from the policy's death benefit.

Bank rate during accumulation · continuous loan balance carries through to refinance
2
Refinance to the insurance company — the balance doesn't reset

At the refinance year, the insurance company pays off the bank. The loan balance carries over intact — only the accrual rate changes, from the bank rate to the carrier's participating loan rate.

—
3
Take income for life, or let the asset grow toward a death benefit

If income is taken, it comes as tax-free policy loans — never reported to the IRS, never subject to a Required Minimum Distribution schedule. If income is never drawn, the same asset instead grows into a substantial illustrated death benefit.
